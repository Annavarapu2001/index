About Me

I'm a backend software engineer with 3+ years of professional experience designing and scaling distributed microservices. My work spans Java/Spring Boot APIs, Kafka-driven event pipelines, and AWS cloud deployments.

At Shop Online I built fault-tolerant payment systems that cut reconciliation failures by 90%. At Capgemini I served an insurance platform with 50K+ daily users and reduced API response times by 75% through deep profiling and query optimization.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Memphis (GPA 3.59) and a B.E. in Electronics & Communication Engineering from Gudlavalleru Engineering College. My dual hardware-software background gives me an unusually grounded perspective on how systems actually behave under pressure.
